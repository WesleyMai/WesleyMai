### 🪖 AI Smart Helmet

<p>
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/source-closed-6B7280?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=white" alt="Closed source" />
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/role-mobile_app-0EA5E9?style=flat-square" alt="Role: mobile app" />
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/platform-Android_%7C_iOS-1E3A8A?style=flat-square" alt="Platform: Android and iOS" />
</p>

A smart safety helmet for motorcycle and e-bike riders, made by **01WeiChuang (零一唯创)**. The software has three parts: a phone app for Android and iOS, a cloud backend, and an admin console. **I build most of the mobile app** in a software team of three.

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🆘 **Fall & SOS alerts** | When the helmet detects a fall, the app counts down and then alerts the rider's emergency contacts |
| 🗺️ **Ride tracking** | Live location, ride history and daily ride summaries on a map |
| 🎧 **Connected riding** | Bluetooth pairing with the helmet, group voice intercom, and push notifications |
